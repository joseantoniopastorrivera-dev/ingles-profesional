This article from The Guardian's TechScape newsletter examines how memes, gaming and internet culture relate to Charlie Kirk's shooting. It explains that the fatal bullet carried a phrase that internet users employ to troll the furry community. According to Know Your Meme, the phrase mocks furries, although furries have also reclaimed it as their own. Furthermore, other bullets referenced an anti-fascist song popular among gamers and bomb commands from the videogame Helldivers 2. The suspect, Tyler James Robinson, is a 22-year-old Utah man accused of killing Kirk at Utah Valley University. At 31, Kirk represented Turning Point USA and was known for debating race, immigration, gender identity and guns online. He was also a key figure in attracting college-aged conservatives to Donald Trump. Moreover, Kirk founded Turning Point USA in 2012 to bring conservative ideas to young people, as Obama once did. He played a central role in the rise of MAGA politics and in the transformation of traditional media. Overall, the text shows how online culture and politics are closely connected in the events surrounding Kirk's death.
